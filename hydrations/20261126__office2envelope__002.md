## 5. Execution Spine discovered, not imposed

The recurring station-shaped activities became visible as an **Execution Spine**:

`survey -> acquire -> cultivate -> exercise -> seal -> reconcile -> reassess`

This is a *recognition over occurrences*, not an obligatory linear scheduler. Stations may repeat, branch, fail, or be approached from another situated Place.

Most candidates can be straightforward Bash macro-type expressors, consuming bounded inputs and emitting reliable residue. Use more sophisticated AMAP worker machinery only where assessment warrants it. An AMAP worker can perform transduction through the proposed 🌱--semdel ontology, consume IntentCapsules, assess compatibility, preservation, and situated opportunity, and emit assessments into CommentaryStream residue.

Distinguish:
- **Execution residue:** branch/status, stdout, diffs, HTTP and runtime responses, commit SHAs.
- **Assessment residue:** recognition, Intent satisfaction judgments, mismatch, alternative route proposals, unknowns, next-Encounter affordances.

The worker or station does not thereby *own* the Encounter. Nor does the Execution Spine become the director of the ecology. Pressure on the station design comes from real worker Journeys and the value of emitted residue, not from a prematurely comprehensive capability taxonomy.

## 6. The critical Envelope provenance correction

This Envelope was historically branched *from* the Office Place branch. That provenance was valid for the actual work and must not be rewritten. But we recognized a hazard in making that a default pattern: an Envelope derived from a privileged participant can inadvertently inherit its assumptions and entangle integration before acquiring other participants.

**Prospective pattern:** establish an Envelope independently from the appropriate accepted baseline; use a Blueprint-like expression to identify the desired coming together, candidate participants and pinned source projections, preservation commitments, traversal tests, and open questions; then explicitly acquire participants and integrate.

The Blueprint is proposed coordination residue, **not yet an implemented schema or execution engine**. Git ancestry preserves provenance; Blueprint-like material positions work and expected relations; hydration records cognitive and historical consequences. The Envelope can be an integration Place and smoke-test pivot. Integration of participants may itself become an Encounter there.

Do not retrofit this independently sourced Envelope rule onto this already published Office branch. Exercise it on a subsequent Journey when its Intent warrants it.
